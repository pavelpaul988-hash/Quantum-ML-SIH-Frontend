ABOUT THE PROJECT

Exploring the future of intelligent health analytics

HealthQML is a student-developed research prototype created around the SIH problem statement IH26139.


The platform combines traditional machine-learning techniques with hybrid quantum-machine-learning experiments to investigate early health-risk classification.
